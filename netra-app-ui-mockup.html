Netra Sahayak — app flow

Built for the person holding the camera, not the person reading the paper.

Three screens, one job each. No menus, no jargon, no login screen to get lost in — a health worker with five minutes of training should be able to run this on the first try, in bright daylight, with no signal.

  • 1One action per screen. Capture, wait, read — never two decisions at once.
  • 2The highlight is the interface. Trust comes from showing the exact spot the model flagged, not a confidence score.
  • 3Offline is the default state, always visible, never something to configure.
  • 4Plain language, not a diagnosis code. "Refer to a doctor" or "Looks healthy" — nothing to interpret.
